About Campus Event Portal

Welcome to the official Campus Event Management & Registration Portal. Our platform is designed to bridge the gap between university management and students by bringing all technical, cultural, and sports events under one roof.

Whether it's a 24-hour coding hackathon, an electrifying annual cultural fest, or an inter-college sports championship, students can easily explore, track dates, and register seamlessly without missing out on campus life updates.

Why Choose Our Portal?

🎯 Centralized Hub

All university events, venues, and schedules are listed neatly in categorized sections.

⚡ Easy Registration

Quick and simple student registration forms with immediate department tracking.

📱 Fully Responsive

Optimized for both desktop and mobile devices so you can access updates on the go.

📌 Project & Committee Info:
This portal is developed as an Internet Programming project to simplify student event participation. All registrations are directly managed by the Student Cultural & Technical Committee.
